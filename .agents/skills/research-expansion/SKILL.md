# Research Expansion — Discover, Derive, Discriminate

**When to activate:** user asks for exploration, a novel idea, a new research direction, explanation of a negative result, or a way out of repetitive local optimization. This skill is proactive: use it for creative research, not only when a process is stalled.

1. **FIND THE OPENING:** state the strongest observation or mathematical constraint and the new question it creates. Place it relative to the project's north star without requiring a terminal-performance explanation from an early idea.
2. **DIVERGE:** sketch distinctive alternatives in information timing, mathematical representation, operator choice, action granularity, causal horizon or training regime. Early sketches may be ambitious; mark assumptions and how they could be falsified.
3. **DERIVE:** choose the most promising unexpected structure. Reconstruct the decision-critical actual computation and derive its action, key equation, counterexample and predicted observable.
4. **DISCRIMINATE:** select a manageable few competing mechanisms for a proof, static inspection, existing-data comparison, or minimal targeted test. The number of experiments can be small even when idea generation is broad.
5. **CONNECT:** explain what this teaches about the long-term question, and describe an eventual terminal bridge when one is already plausible. A new mechanism remains a valid exploration seed when this bridge is open.
6. **ADVANCE:** promote mature ideas to registered experiments with matched controls, resource costs and original success criteria. Preserve exact closed experiment scopes and truthful evidence levels.
7. **LEAVE A USEFUL TRACE:** record an optional lightweight exploration_seed; registered research_moves and measured artifacts use their stricter state fields.

When a better representation or alternative task is the opening, use problem-reformulation skill to construct and discriminate, without turning reformulation into a mandatory gate.

For actual implementation, use mechanism-research skill, core/OBJECT_RECONSTRUCTION.md, core/DEDUCTION_FIRST.md, core/GOAL_ALIGNMENT.md, core/EXPANSION_PROTOCOL.md and on-demand core/PROMPT_PLAYBOOK.md. Discovery is broad; external writes and resource-consuming actions remain authorized and measured.

**Preferred response:** novel mechanism/insight first -> one mathematical or causal derivation -> revealing falsifier/next test -> relationship to the research aim. Keep evidence boundaries concise.
