# Constructive Resolution Loop (CRL)

Based on the adaptive proof exploration and counterexample-driven reformulation in arXiv:2610.05781 Appendix A, independent verification principles in OSVerify, and evaluator-guided search in FunSearch/AlphaEvolve. Adaptation to deep-learning research is a methodological proposal, not a demonstrated transfer of benchmark gains.

**Core invariant:** decision-relevant uncertainty can seed new explanatory mechanisms, followed by the most informative scientific action. Discovering a possible confounder is not completion of a task.

Routes:
- MAP_FLOW: inspect missing source, tensor or state edge.
- DERIVE: prove a result, derive a bound, or construct a counterexample.
- STATIC_CHECK: verify implementation-to-equation correspondence.
- EXPERIMENT: measure a necessary data-dependent or system-level unknown.
- DESIGN: introduce a mechanism-derived intervention with falsifiable predictions.
- ACCEPT_CONDITIONAL: scoped mathematical question resolved without redundant experiments.
- REPAIR/PIVOT: independently established defect prompts local repair or new representation.
- DEFER: specify an exact unavailable dependency or authorization boundary.

During **ideation**, allow many lightweight mechanisms and even preliminary conditional conjectures. When selecting a **discriminating test portfolio**, focus on two or three promising mechanisms with distinct causal paths and competing predictions. Prefer minimum-cost tests that change which mechanism is believed or which method should be built. Do not invent precise information-gain probabilities.

Criticism is not a veto unless substantive: state the inference challenged, actual evidence, and the proof/counterexample/intervention that resolves it. Store failed branches with their assumptions, concrete obstruction and lessons; avoid repeated unsuccessful searches.

Evidence distinctions: proposed mechanism != source-confirmed computation != proved conditional theorem != measured observation. A queued job is not a completed experiment.

Reports begin with the new scientific insight, constructive mechanism or strongest affirmative result. Present a discriminating prediction or exact conditional boundary, then the action that advances the question. Include concise evidence status instead of dominating the response with generic caveats.

**Two passes, not two barriers:** divergent conjecture generation may precede complete source reconstruction, provided assumptions remain visible. Later convergence checks the real computation, theorem scope, experiment controls and authorization. A closed candidate contributes information without forbidding a distinct scientific seed.
