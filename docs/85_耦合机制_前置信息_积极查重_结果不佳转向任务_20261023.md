## First principles: five forms of coupling must not be confused

1. **Intra-FFN additive decomposition.** Given a frozen pre-W1 input, weights, and shared constants, the exact real-valued GELU FFN is a sum of hidden group contributions. Empirically recheck HiF4/Q2 group quantization, BF16 reductions, bias, alpha and R256. Changing full-action compensation can introduce set dependence.
2. **Action-value coupling.** Even with additive output increments `d_g,d_h`, squared-loss interaction is `-2 <d_g,d_h>` (with the correct reference and metric). Do not call this nonlinear neuron computation.
3. **Cross-block propagation coupling.** Norm/residual/attention/next FFN modify directions. R7D1 already proves certain local/downstream rankings disagree on its fixed two-layer scope; it does not prove general strong nonlinear synergy. Reuse the exact four-arm `Y0,YA,YB,YAB` decomposition.
4. **Cross-step/spatiotemporal coupling.** Current token-wise FFN perturbations can spread through future attention/denoising and alter lighting, background and motion. R16 temporal routing geometry is a hint, not a causal demonstration.
5. **Information overlap and policy feedback.** Earlier-layer features can be redundant given actual post-Q1 input; sparse actions change later states. A dense-prefix oracle is not a free sparse-student policy.

**New structural seed, to be falsified:** the post-LayerNorm/AdaLN/Q1 representation can hide residual-state scale and update history, yet later normalized residual propagation depends on those hidden variables. Evaluate `residual_std`, pre-norm residual summary, and already-computed attention increment as pre-W1 extra information; account for making a fused norm statistic available. This is not an asserted Wan observation.

For complete native actions `a`, conditioned on affordable pre-W1 information `Z`, measure
`J(Z) = E[max_a E[U_a|Z]]`,
`NetVOI(S|Z)=J(Z,S)-J(Z)-C_observe(S)`.
Use *compensation-aware finite downstream loss improvement* `U_a`, not only hidden magnitude; charge selector and adaptation overhead. Conditional mutual information is diagnostic, not a substitute for decision improvement.
