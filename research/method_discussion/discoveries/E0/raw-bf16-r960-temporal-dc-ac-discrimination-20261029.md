# Does rank-960 preserve temporal innovation?

**OPEN:** A low-rank W2 fitted to uncentered saved teacher outputs may allocate substantial capacity to persistent image appearance. The observed combination of high static consistency and lower RAFT motion makes it useful to test whether time-varying FFN output is disproportionately distorted. This is a competing mechanism, not a conclusion about why the free-running videos differ.

**ALG:** At one fixed source, denoising step, block and spatial anchor, let `aτ = g ⊙ Yτ`, where `Yτ` is the actual Dense W2 output including b2 and `g` is the captured gate. Let `âτ` be the corresponding shared- or step-r960 output on the *same Dense hτ*. For each of the 21 latent-time positions, define `μ = meanτ aτ`, `Aτ = aτ − μ` and likewise `μ̂, Âτ` for the candidate. The mean energy and temporal-innovation energy are `21||μ||²` and `Στ||Aτ||²`; the analogous candidate quantities give separate DC and AC energy ratios. Report a ratio only when its Dense denominator is positive, otherwise mark it unavailable. These axes refer to latent-video time τ within one denoising step, not differences between the four denoising steps.

For the actual output error `eτ = âτ − aτ`, temporal centering gives the exact identity `Στ||eτ||² = 21||meanτ eτ||² + Στ||eτ − meanτ eτ||²`. Report both terms normalized by the corresponding Dense DC or AC energy, plus the total gated output error. For AC, also report `Στ<Aτ,Âτ>/Στ||Aτ||²` and cosine where defined. An energy ratio near one alone is insufficient: `||Â||² = ||A||² + ||Â−A||² + 2<A,Â−A>`, so an incorrect direction or phase can preserve total energy. Record signed cross terms, source-level values and tails; average anchors/positions within source before equal-source summaries.

**SRC:** The actual frozen factor forward in `tools/raw_bf16_full80_spectrum.py` performs BF16 `mm(h,left.T)` followed by BF16 `addmm(b2,middle,right.T)`; it is not an exact continuous rank-r multiplication. `postwrite` applies the actual typed gate and BF16 residual addition. The new collector saves, at each actual source/step/block, 168 Dense teacher rows arranged as 21τ×8 fixed spatial anchors, including `h`, `Y`, `r`, `u` and gate. The anchors are static image locations, not motion-aligned tracks. These are pending acquisition assets until their actual registered producer and independent audit exit successfully.

**Distinguishing test:** On audited packets after acquisition, apply the already exported shared80 and step160 factors to the same captured Dense `h`, retaining original b2. Compare captured Dense `Y`, both actual BF16 factor results, continuous gated DC/AC geometry and actual typed postwrite `û` versus captured `u`. At fixed h, a disproportionate AC error or lower aligned AC gain relative to DC would support temporal-innovation loss at this boundary; comparable AC/DC accuracy would weaken this mechanism. Keep all source/step/block/anchor tails, and treat near-zero AC energy as undefined rather than inflating a ratio. Source-specific gates and actual row IDs must be checked rather than assumed.

A bounded full-ten-source producer could stream exactly 1,600 audited packets, load the fixed 80 shared and 160 step factor files, and perform **3,200 factor forwards / 6,400 BF16 factor GEMMs** on 168 saved Dense rows, plus the registered postwrite and scalar reductions. No W1, full model, fit or video is needed. To distinguish M168 GEMM rounding from factor approximation, a separately costed control can replay original W2+b2 once per packet on the same h and report its error against captured full-M `Y`; this adds 1,600 original BF16 GEMMs and the 80 original W2/bias assets, and is not a free baseline. Freeze input/output byte and time limits, actual parent exits, hashes, device/precision, source role and all counts before running. A smaller fixed-anchor pilot can precede the full all-site producer if separately registered.

The strongest alternatives are (1) the factors are adequate on Dense h but free-running student states drift across steps, changing later h and attention; and (2) scene texture or camera/image content changes the RAFT score without corresponding action loss. Other candidates include true AC error already introduced before W2 and downstream gate/residual/norm or attention effects. Same-Dense-h replay separates local function error from student-state drift but does not settle the latter. Counterfactual student-state capture/intervention and matched video inspection are needed to distinguish those explanations, with full 21τ trajectories and human action/camera assessment treated as separate evidence. No new real tensor, GEMM, model or GPU was executed for this note.
