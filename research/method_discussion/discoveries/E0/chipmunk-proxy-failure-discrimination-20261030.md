# Chipmunk FFN paid-proxy failure: bounded mechanism preflight

Status: static source/header/metadata audit, not an experiment or approval. The previously measured source898/high/block20/step0→1 group has proxy K5120 gated relative-L2 0.279554 versus 0.309–0.321 for equal-K random/fixed controls; full update 0.002986 and empty update 0.448862. This shows useful ranking and a substantial remaining local error in one non-raster teacher group. The prior same-M128 dense initializer is part of that experiment's identity; it is not full-M replay or free-running student evidence.

## Competing explanations

1. **Group mean loses heterogeneity through GELU.** The paid score is the absolute BF16 difference between `F.linear(mean(V1_1), W1, b1)` and `mean(F.linear(V1_0,W1,b1))`, before GELU, see `tools/e0_chipmunk_paid_proxy_probe.py:348–360`. A small signed mean can hide large individual changes and nonlinear response: a two-row pre-GELU group `(+t,-t)` has mean zero for every t but its mean GELU varies with t. Both state changes and the row distribution matter, not just the group's preactivation mean.
2. **The proxy tracks changes, but consumer direction/cache dominates.** Even if the proxy ranks changes in h well, omitted columns can align strongly with `gate × W2`, or signed cross-column contributions can reinforce; the cached historical h/Y, BF16 rounding and shape-dependent replay may add their own differences. The output error is not the sum of independent channel energies. The old report's already persisted 13,824 scores, masks, per-arm h/Y/gated errors and signed cross support only the observed local ranking and numerical-control conclusions; they do not separate these mechanisms.
