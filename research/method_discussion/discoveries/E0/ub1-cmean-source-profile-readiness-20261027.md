# C_mean source-cluster profile: original computation and readiness

**[SRC/OBS]** The frozen 24-source support is an equal-source average of gated **signed group-output energy**. The existing four-site saved operands make source sensitivity measurable without generating new video or fitting a model. The immediate discriminator is whether leaving one image cluster out changes the fixed whole-64 support or its boundary margin at step 0/block 39 and three predeclared controls; the result would describe stability conditional on the existing 24×256 sampled rows, not establish calibration sufficiency.

For source `s`, selected saved row `r`, and group `j`, the original score is

`q[s,r,j] = sum_d float64((float32(V2[s,r,Gj]) @ float32(W2[:,Gj]).T) * float32(gate[d]))²`.

The multiply by gate is performed in FP32 **before** conversion to FP64 and the output-coordinate sum. [generate_proxy_pr2.py](/home/xiang/projects/FFNDS/tools/generate_proxy_pr2.py:18) reshapes the 216 contiguous 64-channel groups and executes 27 `torch.bmm` calls of eight groups per source, using each native effective W2 value converted to FP32. [fit_cstatic_centered.py](/home/xiang/projects/FFNDS/tools/fit_cstatic_centered.py:141) computes the row mean per source, then adds it divided by 24 in source order. [fit_step_budget.py](/home/xiang/projects/FFNDS/tools/fit_step_budget.py:113) consumes this original stored `C_mean`; step 0/3 support sizes are 54/162, with stable lower-group-index tie handling. `ar2_core.score_families` also computes signed output before its norm; the E family instead squares contributions before summation and is a different score.

The [matched-recovery implementation](/home/xiang/projects/FFNDS/tools/ub1_recovery_math.py:49) uses a separate FP64 Gram formulation, and its saved even/odd fold vectors are means over 12 sources. The identity is valid in real arithmetic, but its floating-point path and reduction order differ from the original FP32 BMM. The completed matched-recovery RUN retains two 216-value aggregate fold vectors per position, not 24 source vectors. Original static-fit checkpoints retain the pooled `C_mean`. Neither aggregate identifies the individual vectors needed for leave-one-source-out or source bootstrap; missing vectors must not be inferred from those means.
