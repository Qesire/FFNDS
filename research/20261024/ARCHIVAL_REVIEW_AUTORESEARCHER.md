# FFNDS — AutoResearcher lifecycle and archival re-audit · 2026-10-24

## Research decision

The risk was not lack of advice but excess historical **task authority**: old dated research guides (e.g. R4/R5 rejection vetoes, AR2 restart, R15 execution instruction, C2 retry and old goal hierarchy) lived beside today's science. This encouraged automatic copying of an outdated route instead of re-deriving a mechanism from source evidence. The solution is **live scientific objectives + historical evidence + archived directives**, with explicit transitions between them.

### AutoResearcher principles applied

- **PRD / problem-reformulation:** changed the unit of audit from deleting “negative results” to distinguishing *claims, evidence and live instructions*. A failed R4 action remains evidence; its global “forbid this family” rhetoric does not become a current rule.
- **MEAR / research-expansion:** retain the possibility that old failed methods expose new information, algebraic decomposition, causal directions or operator granularity. Do not make archived status a permission gate for ideas.
- **CR/DF/CRL / mechanism-research:** retain actual numerical evidence, source hashes, registered protocols and paper-level mechanism details at their original scope. Archive only superseded *presentation and directions*, not the evidence needed to verify a mathematical or performance claim.
