The UB1 and compact results show why multiple quality axes and actual motion matter: imaging may remain stable while movement falls. RAFT is a flow statistic, not an action-semantic oracle; latent SSE cannot determine motion preservation. Full-play human review, action/ROI, camera and fair latency remain open. HiF4, original BF16 and NVFP4 use different checkpoint identities and must not share a Dense denominator.

For the next method comparison, freeze a full160 original-BF16 candidate and its calibration sources before observing outcomes; pair it with original-BF16 Dense on at least 20 distinct development image clusters, covering applicable action, background, static and camera strata. Match image, prompt, actual noise, scheduler and seed; prioritize one genuine paired video per source, then add an independent second seed. Predeclare tolerances and tail criteria for applicable visual, motion and semantic axes; record balanced cold/warm wall, complete FFN, observer, packing, offload and memory costs. A disjoint confirmation cohort is a separate preregistered step; 48 clusters is a target, not a claim of availability. Five-sample official claims require five genuine independent generations per condition.
