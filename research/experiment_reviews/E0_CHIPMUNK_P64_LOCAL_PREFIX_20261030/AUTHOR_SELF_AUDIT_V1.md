## Scientific question and discriminating predictions

The closed full40 observation acquisition found lower pooled same-state gated relative L2 for source979 (0.342973) than source898 (0.360532), despite worse previously measured motion and MUSIQ for979. Aggregate local FFN error alone does not rank these two exposed source/seed42 videos. The new CPU scope compares the same saved row and gate across free Dense and student trajectories, without generating another video or evaluating a model.

Let `Y_D` be free-Dense output, `Y_S*` the paid Dense shadow on current student input, and `Y_S` the actual student output. Compute local action difference `L=Y_S-Y_S*`, prefix-state output difference `P=Y_S*-Y_D`, and total difference `T=Y_S-Y_D`. The algebraic identity is `T=L+P`, hence `||T||²=||L||²+||P||²+2<L,P>`. FP64 implementations report explicit identity residuals and fixed tolerances. This is not a bitwise floating-point identity. Positive and negative cross terms can respectively reinforce or cancel. Their ratios are descriptive terms, not causal percentages.

One explanation predicts spatial/latent-time concentration of local error; another predicts a different ranking after prefix-state differences and their signed alignment are included. Both can hold. Growth of P is not H3 causal amplification: cross-trajectory residuals R are not collected, so the analysis does not decompose actual cross-trajectory residual writes, downstream attention, flow, or final quality causally. A subsequent intervention would need a separate scope.
