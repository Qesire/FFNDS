For the same grid row, output channel and shared gate, define `L = Y_S − Y_S*`, `P = Y_S* − Y_D`, `T = Y_S − Y_D`, with Y_S the actual student candidate, Y_S* the Dense shadow on the student input, and Y_D the free-Dense output. The identity and signed cross distinguish local action discrepancy from the output difference associated with distinct trajectory states. L/P may reinforce or cancel; their squared-energy ratios can exceed one and are not causal contribution percentages. FP64 arithmetic has finite rounding, so the implementation reports and checks vector and energy identity residuals against fixed tolerances; the extreme-exponent counterexample is included in software fixtures.

A large P can arise from accumulated previous actions and the nonlinear mapping of their states without showing downstream causal amplification; even a small L or P may have a sensitive direction. These observations cannot identify H3, explain video quality causally, or establish cross-trajectory actual BF16 residual writes, because R_D and R_S are not captured. The two previously exposed sources and seed42 are the independent source units; rows, blocks and τ/quadrants are nested. Evaluation256 excludes selector64, and inverse inclusion probabilities target the eligible population conditional on that exclusion. Selector64 and full-M are reported separately. The four full-M blocks are preselected and cannot exclude anomalies elsewhere or guarantee small-object coverage.

The proposed producer requires both actual native and independent 310-artifact verification EXITs, and checks that the verifier's parent RUN/EXIT bindings are exactly the current native RUN/EXIT. It checks parent cache sample and four snapshot identities and block0 current-input identities, and validates all 40 sites' gate and coordinates before reading Y payloads. Sample target spans are rehashed individually; full-M singleton files are checked for whole-file and raw SHA in one pass, with offset0 and no trailing bytes. Parent independent verification supplies the earlier complete sample-file SHA; this CPU scope does not reread unselected sample tensors. If source identity, finite values, numeric closure or resource limits fail, the scope stops without automatic retry.

Adversarial counterexample: a rare object omitted by 256 rows or located outside the four retained full-M blocks can carry the quality-relevant discrepancy even when these aggregates appear stable. Conversely, large P and signed cross can be mapped away downstream. A fixed-prefix perturbation and separate video-quality study would be needed to discriminate H3; this scope cannot grant that conclusion.
