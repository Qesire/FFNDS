# Whole-M causal proxy: initial author review

Question: on a single shared K5120 mask across 31,668 real raster rows, does increasing causally paid sample information from four to 64 rows approach the post-reference full-RMS oracle, and do those rankings leave sufficient same-M FFN accuracy and physical headroom? The two competing predictions are (1) P64 closes much of the P4→oracle gap, indicating limited sample information; (2) P64 remains far from oracle, or P64 approaches oracle while the oracle remains inaccurate or costly, indicating respectively information-estimation or action-unit/consumer limits. The latter is not a proof about a different groupwise action. Four scopes are two exposed image sources × two expert step pairs, not four independent images.

Each scope binds the same two passive full-M captures and original BF16 expert checkpoint. Previous full-M W1/GELU/W2 plus previous preactivation mean are separately timed cache construction. A separately charged random-mask full-M call compiles/warms the selected kernels. Four online arms use mean / P4 / P64 / random, all K5120; P4 and P64 use one seed42 actual-row permutation and prefix 4/64. Each online CUDA event and synchronized wall encloses input reduction/gather, M1/M4/M64 proxy W1 and GELU when applicable, stable selector, index conversion, full cache clone, selected W1/GELU, selected W2/cache add, and BF16 current gate. All ledger updates, output writes and reporting are outside that timing region. The M31668 dense reference includes full W1/GELU/W2 and gate. After all online arms, full-current h becomes available and a separately charged RMS oracle ranking and physical selected action run; it is not an online selector.

Both previous and current reconstructed full-M h/Y are compared to the saved actual tensors. Since captures and this proposed worker may use different devices/backend algorithms, mismatch is reported rather than silently called byte replay. Snapshot, full-file hashes, logical tensor reads, old-cache construction, first JIT/compilation and reference/oracle overhead are explicit. Peak full-M intermediate memory, 64-row gather/selection cost and the actual H/Y resident cache must be measured. One-shot events on exposed sources do not establish end-to-end speed, video quality or source generalization. This scope does not loop 248 spatial tiles, and repeated 128-row panels are never used as full-M.

Per part operation budget is 2 dense W1/2 GELU/2 dense W2, three proxy W1s and two proxy GELUs, 4 online selectors, 6 selected W1 and 6 selected W2 calls (cold setup + 4 online + oracle), 6 gate products (4 online + dense + oracle), and 1 post-reference oracle selection; source and operand I/O have separate limits. The current protocols are draft NOT_BOUND: concrete worker profiles, GPU indices/launcher specs and actual review identities are not yet fixed. The program has not read a real tensor or invoked GPU/model. Independent reviewer should attack true input/row identity, event/ledger boundary and overlap, native BF16 path, same-M reference, all physical and observer costs, context constraints and failure receipts before registration.
