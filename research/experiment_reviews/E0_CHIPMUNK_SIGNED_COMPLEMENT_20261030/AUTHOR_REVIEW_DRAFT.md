# Signed P64 complement: author self-review, draft

Hypothesis: a common signed omitted-channel change can be estimated from the already paid P64 probe and recovered by one BF16-input, FP32-output W2 GEMV and a broadcast add. If row-common omitted change dominates, sample and separately paid full-row omitted-mean hindsight correction should both improve the same-K packed baseline, with material DC baseline error. If mostly row-specific or P64 sample noise dominates, the hindsight correction and/or causal estimate will give little benefit or the sample can worsen error. These are competing predictions, not quality claims.

Each source/expert pair uses the same 64 predetermined matching rows. In both arms one 64-row W1/GELU produces both RMS selection and FP32 signed mean; the baseline deliberately pays for the unused signed mean, then the P64 selected entries are zeroed, the complement mean is BF16-cast, and the real W2 GEMV uses BF16 inputs with FP32 output. Its result is added to previous pre-gate Y and selected W2 delta before one BF16 output conversion and the current gate. First cold plus three measured invocations each are used for baseline and causal correction, and the same schedule for whole-M Dense. All online work, including full cache clones and SHA-based mask identity check, is inside the CUDA and host intervals. The guard cost is reported separately, but retained in the total. A separately charged frozen packed action on the same mask directly checks the baseline output (relative L2 and max absolute difference); baseline-to-Dense error is also compared with the already closed P64 result; the mask SHA must match. No complete current activation is computed until both online arms finish; the later full-row signed mean is paid and is only a hindsight reference.

The DC/AC decomposition uses the full gate-only output error and FP64 arithmetic; its finite closure difference is reported, not assumed zero. Two old development sources contain two nested expert pairs each; no new video, full160 quality, or generalized result is implied. The extra complement GEMV still reads full W2 and its real cost is included. FP32 output mm is documented on this Torch build, not yet proved on A100. Unsupported execution is a preserved failure without fallback. The four bound plans remain REVIEW_REQUESTED without ACTIVE authorization; real CPU and GPU attempts require independent exact recheck, registered scopes, and actual preflight EXIT0.

For a fixed support and real linear W2/gate, projecting the full omitted activation mean equals the row mean of its omitted output, so the best constant correction removes that idealized DC component. Here the same 64 rows choose S by RMS and estimate the signed mean, so adaptive selection couples the estimate and mask; fixed-S unbiasedness cannot be claimed. The one frozen seed tests realized behavior and sampling noise can worsen it. BF16 rounding means the projected full omitted mean can differ from the true mean of the measured gated output error; hindsight is not asserted to be the global optimal constant correction. Direct baseline-error DC/AC is a separately paid post-reference diagnostic.
