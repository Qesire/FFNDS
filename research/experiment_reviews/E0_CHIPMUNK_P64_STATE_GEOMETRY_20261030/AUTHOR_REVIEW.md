# Paired saved-state geometry: author self-review

This CPU scope uses two already independently verified V2 source trajectories (898 and 96), original Dense and global-P64, one seed each. For every saved denoising step it reports flow and post-step latent differences, with the original full input state as the reference. Step 0 flow and latent have explicit file byte comparisons; differences are reported as a control observation and do not trigger a causal claim. The initial noise pair must be byte-identical before any tensor decoding. Parent producer and independent verifier actual EXIT0, all 36 source state bindings and raw tensor hashes are required. No video, model, GPU, training or scoring runs.

For error D[channel, latent-time, height, width], DC is each channel's mean over latent-time and space; T is the per-latent-time spatial mean minus DC; S is the spatial residual. Float64 squared energies and each signed doubled pairwise cross are retained with a numerical closure check. T describes temporal changes of spatial means, S includes all spatial structure and its variation in latent time: neither is labelled subject motion or flicker. Four denoising steps are separate from the 21 latent-time samples; latent axes are not individual decoded video frames. Dense energy is the denominator for relative SSE/L2, with explicit null on zero. The two source-level relative SSEs are equally averaged per step, rather than treating positions or steps as independent sources.

H1 predicts most trajectory divergence already after the high-noise second step; H2 predicts a substantial later increase at the final low-noise step. These are descriptive comparisons: later states contain prior student drift, so a step2/3 difference cannot isolate the newly applied action. A later high-only/low-only native intervention would be needed for causal attribution. Five NumPy synthetic tests verify the decomposition, zero denominator, malformed shape, failure-progress retention and rejection of unlimited address space; a separate tiny NumPy import passed with CUDA hidden and a 2 GiB address-space limit.

The exact known tensor/input reads are precharged and completed per operation, including one file SHA per state, actual initial and step0 byte comparisons and one raw read per flow/latent; source, NumPy binary and interpreter SHA plus parent protocol/RUN/EXIT/VERIFY byte reads are separately precharged in the same 512 MiB budget. Small metadata and OS/runtime loader reads outside those explicit operations are UNKNOWN; this is not a kernel-level I/O counter. Output total is sampled at charged boundaries and at completion, not a hard filesystem quota. The externally applied 2 GiB RLIMIT_AS is stronger than a 2 GiB RSS cap; wall and timeout apply to preflight and analysis. A force-kill may leave an incomplete receipt and the attempt remains consumed.
