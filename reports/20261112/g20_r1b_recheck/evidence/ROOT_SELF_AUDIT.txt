1. PROBLEM
R1 reported that the free-ridge arm A = A_Q1 + D degrades DEV5 math-lift MSE at
all six expensive sites, while its own certificates showed D numerical ranks
4239-4732. The registered objective is a source-equal-weight gated ridge with
per-column filter g_j^2/(g_j^2*mu + rho). Its EXACT minimizer satisfies
rank(D) <= rank(X_CAL) <= 517 (high) / 523 (low), because b_j = X^T W R lies in
range(M) = range(X^T X) and (g_j^2 M + rho I)^{-1} preserves range(M). The
published ranks violate this bound by ~8-9x. R1 formed the Gram M and RHS B in
FP32 (fit_core.py L103-117) and only lifted to F64 at the filter/eigh stage.
FP32 formation injects null-space components into B of relative size ~1e-7,
which the filter amplifies by g_j^2/rho ~ 1e6-1e7 (rho = 1.4e-7..2.9e-7 at the
six sites). The resulting D is therefore NOT the exact minimizer of the
registered objective; it is a noise-amplified artifact whose DEV behavior
cannot be attributed to the objective itself.

2. COMPETING PREDICTIONS (the experiment must discriminate)
H_artifact: the DEV degradation is mostly the FP32 null-space noise. Prediction:
a certified exact solve (D in the CAL row space, rank <= 517/523) recovers DEV5
MSE near Q1 or better, while the recomputed original D shows large
out-of-row-space energy and reproduces the published degradation.
H_overfit: the registered objective genuinely overfits ~500 CAL rows for a
5120x5120 D even when solved exactly. Prediction: the certified exact solve
still degrades DEV5 substantially vs Q1, with small out-of-row-space energy of
the original D relative to its total (i.e. the in-space part alone overfits).
Mixed outcomes are possible and will be reported quantitatively, not forced
into either narrative. omega (DEV out-of-CAL-row-space energy fraction) bounds
how much of any DEV change the fit can even explain.
