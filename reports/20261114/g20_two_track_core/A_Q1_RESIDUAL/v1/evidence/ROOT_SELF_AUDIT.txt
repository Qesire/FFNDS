3. BASES, CENTER/SCALE, FEATURES (A2.2-A2.3)
P64 = top-64 right singular vectors of weighted stacked X (F64 thin SVD), w_s=1/(11*512).
P32=P64[:,:32]. Q32 = top-32 right singular vectors of weighted stacked PRE-GATE residual
(Y - X@A_q1) — NOT the gated residual. This is the rev5 fixed coordinate choice; the true
gate weights remain in the fit_readout normal equations. No DEV in any basis.
b = sum_s w_s*(h0.sum(0)+h1.sum(0))/2 with h0=U0@P32,h1=U1@P32; sigma = pooled endpoint
std around b, clamp_min(1e-6). Fixed tanh (no GELU/SiLU, no temperature search).
Raw features (each 64 cols): L=X@P64; D=[X@P32, tanh((X@P32)/sigma)] (no b offset, odd);
E=[X@P32, tanh((U1@P32-b)/sigma)-tanh((U0@P32-b)/sigma)]. D and E first-32 columns are the
identical X.double()@P32. Per-arm gamma_j=sqrt(sum_s w_s sum_rows Phi_raw^2).clamp_min(1e-6);
Phi=Phi_raw/gamma; no feature-mean subtraction (preserves zero-increment/antisymmetry).

4. READOUT SOLVER (A3) — V4 fit_readout body verbatim, base A_q1

READOUT_EPS=1e-6 (unchanged relative Tikhonov; not 1e-3, not DEV-tuned). vec(C)=solve(K+rho I,vec(T)).

relative residual required <=1e-8 (SOLVED branch). No second full SVD/eigh solver.
Objective J(C)=sum_s w_s||g*(X A_q1+Phi C Q32^T-Y)||_F^2+rho||C||_F^2.
